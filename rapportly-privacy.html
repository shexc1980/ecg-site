Rapportly privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026. Last updated November 7, 2026.

Rapportly helps you remember what matters about the people in your life and reminds you before the dates that matter to them. The app is published by Elevon Capital Group LLC, a California company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who helps us run the app, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Rapportly app and to emails it sends. Our website has its own website notice.

The short version

  • We use your information only to run Rapportly for you.
  • We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and no location tracking.
  • You can export your data or delete your account in the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us.

  • Account: your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time link and to send your reminders, and your confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • The people you add: names, relationships, groups, birthdays and other dates, the facts and notes you type or record about them, budgets, gifts you log and the gift ideas you save.
  • Voice notes: recordings you choose to make so they can be turned into text.
  • Settings: your notification and reminder preferences.
  • Messages to us: what you send when you contact support.

Information from your device, with your permission.

  • Microphone: used only while you are recording a voice note.
  • Contacts: read only if you choose to import contacts, and only to add the people you select.
  • Notifications: a push token from your device that lets us deliver your reminders.

Information created in the app. Text transcribed from your voice notes, facts the app suggests from your notes (saved only if you confirm them), gift ideas, and the record of reminders sent.

We do not collect your location, browsing activity, advertising identifiers or payment card details, and we do not buy information about you or the people you add from data brokers or other outside sources.

2. Information about other people

Rapportly stores details about people in your life because you enter them. Please add only what you are comfortable keeping and what you have a right to share. This information is private to your account: we never contact, profile, search for or share information about the people you add, and we never combine it with outside data. Sensitive details you choose to save (for example, about someone's health or faith) get the same protection and are used only to provide the app to you. If you believe information about you was stored in Rapportly, you can contact us at the address below.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the app: store your data, sync it, and sign you in.
  • To send the reminders and emails you set up.
  • To turn voice notes into text, suggest facts from your notes, and suggest gift ideas based on what you have saved.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse, enforce usage limits and fix problems.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

4. How AI is used

Rapportly uses AI services for three tasks: transcribing voice notes, suggesting facts from your notes, and suggesting gift ideas. The app asks for your permission before it first sends your notes to these services, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings, then AI features; while it is off, these three features do not run. The text of the note or the facts involved are sent to the AI service for processing only. The providers we use do not train their models on this data and are set not to keep it after processing (Groq with zero data retention, and Deepgram with model-improvement data use turned off). Suggestions are only suggestions: nothing is added to your records until you confirm it, and gift ideas are based only on facts you have saved.

6. Store links

Gift ideas can include links to stores such as Amazon, Etsy or Walmart. Some are paid links and are labelled "Paid link"; we may earn a commission at no cost to you, and paid links never decide which ideas you see. We do not send your information to the store. Once you open a store's website, that store's privacy policy applies.

7. How long we keep information

  • Your account and the data in it: while your account is open. You can delete any item at any time.
  • Voice-note audio: deleted right after transcription, unless you turn on "Keep voice recordings" (in Settings, then Notifications, or for a single person). If a transcription fails, the audio is deleted automatically within about an hour. The transcribed text stays with the note until you delete it.
  • Deleted accounts: kept for a 30-day recovery period in case you change your mind, then permanently deleted. Copies can remain in encrypted database backups for up to 7 more days until those backups expire.
  • Support emails: kept as long as needed to help you and for our records.
  • Service provider records (for example, email delivery logs) are kept for the limited periods set by those providers.

We may keep information longer only where the law requires it.

8. Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each account can reach only its own data, a rule enforced by the database itself. Access to production systems is limited to what is needed to run the app, and keys are kept out of the app's code. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

9. Your choices and controls

  • See, edit or remove any person, fact, note or gift in the app.
  • Export a copy of your data: Settings, then Account, then Export JSON or Export CSV.
  • Delete your account: Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. Signing back in within 30 days restores it; after that it is permanently deleted. You can also ask us by email from the address on your account.
  • AI features: allow or turn off in Settings, then AI features.
  • Withdraw permissions for the microphone, contacts or notifications at any time in your phone's settings, and change reminder settings in the app.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including California and other US states with privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and get a copy in a portable format;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your information;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, targeted advertising and profiling (we do none of these);
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information (we use it only to provide the app, as described above); and
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

How to make a request. Most requests can be completed directly in the app (see section 9). You can also email support@elevoncapitalgroup.com. To protect your account, we verify a request by confirming it came from the email address on the account, and we may ask you to confirm it from that address. An authorized agent may make a request for you with your signed permission; we may ask you to verify your identity directly. We respond within the time the law requires, usually 45 days.

Appeals. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision with "Appeal" in the subject line. We will answer within the time the law requires, and if you disagree with the result you may contact your state attorney general.

Privacy signals. Because we do not sell or share personal information or use it for targeted advertising, there is nothing for a Global Privacy Control or "Do Not Track" signal to turn off; we treat all users as opted out.

11. Age requirement

Rapportly is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we delete it. Information that an adult user enters about their own children is protected like all other data.

12. Where information is processed

Rapportly is offered in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date. If a change materially affects how we use information you have already given us, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
